I'm Animesh Bangal, a Junior in Mechanical Engineering at Louisiana State University with a deep passion for robotics and aerospace systems. My work sits at the intersection of dynamics, control theory, and embedded systems — where mathematics meets the physical world.

From launching payloads into near-space aboard high-altitude balloons to designing intelligent controllers for autonomous drones, I am driven by the challenge of making machines that are robust, precise, and autonomous. 03 — Research

Active Research
Projects.

In Progress
EDMDc-Based Predictive Control for Real-Time Drone Interception

Applying Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition with control (EDMDc) to learn a linear Koopman operator representation of nonlinear drone dynamics. The learned model is used within a Model Predictive Control framework to enable real-time interception of autonomous aerial targets with minimal computational overhead.

Explore the project
In Progress
Desensitized i-PID for Precise Motion Control in UAVs

Extending the intelligent PID (i-PID) framework with a desensitization approach to reduce sensitivity to parameter uncertainty and external disturbances. The work targets high-precision motion control for UAV platforms where classical PID tuning is insufficient under varying payload and environmental conditions.

Explore the project
Summer 2026 · Upcoming
Autonomous Quadcopter for Environmental Water Sampling

A Gulf Scholar project building an autonomous quadcopter that can navigate to a target body of water and collect samples for environmental monitoring. Built on a Pixhawk flight controller paired with a Raspberry Pi companion computer for higher-level autonomy.
